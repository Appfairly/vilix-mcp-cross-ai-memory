# Example: Basic Cross-Tool Memory Workflow

This walks through the core Vilix value: **save context once, reuse it
everywhere.**

## Scenario

You are an AI power user who works across ChatGPT and Claude and is tired of
re-explaining your background every session.

## Step 1 — Establish context in ChatGPT

In a ChatGPT session connected to Vilix:

> "I'm building a side project called Lumen — a habit tracker for makers.
> My priorities right now are onboarding and retention. Remember this."

Vilix saves the meaningful context (project name, what it is, current
priorities).

## Step 2 — Continue in Claude, days later

Open a fresh Claude conversation connected to Vilix:

> "What were my current priorities for my side project, and suggest three
> onboarding improvements."

Claude retrieves the Lumen context from Vilix and answers without you
re-explaining anything.

## Step 3 — Refine and it stays current

> "Update my priorities: onboarding is done, now focus is monetization."

The next tool you open already knows monetization is the current focus.

## What just happened

- You never re-pasted your project background.
- Two different models shared one consistent memory of you.
- You stayed in control — context can be reviewed, edited, or erased anytime
  from the dashboard.
